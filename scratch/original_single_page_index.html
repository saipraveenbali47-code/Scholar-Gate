★ Flagship Schemes

Featured Flagship Scholarships

Key pre-matric government assistance programs with high coverage and impact across AP, TS, and All India.

All India • Merit-cum-Means

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMSS)

Ministry of Education, Govt of India

Annual Stipend ₹12,000 / year
Classes: Classes 8 to 12
Max Income: ₹3,50,000/yr
Beneficiary: Rural & EWS Students
Andhra Pradesh • State Welfare

AP JnanaBhumi Pre-Matric Scholarship

Social & BC Welfare Depts, Govt of AP

Annual Support ₹1,000 – ₹3,500 / year
Classes: Classes 5 to 10
Target: SC / ST / BC / Minorities
Portal: jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in
Telangana • State Welfare

Telangana ePASS Pre-Matric Scholarship

Welfare Departments, Govt of Telangana

Annual Support Up to ₹3,500 / year
Classes: Classes 5 to 10
Target: SC / ST / BC / Disabled
Portal: telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in
All India • DBT Welfare

PM YASASVI Pre-Matric Scholarship

Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, GoI

Direct DBT Allowance ₹4,000 / year
Classes: Classes 9 & 10
Max Income: ₹2,50,000/yr
Target: OBC / EBC / DNT Students
