      About SCHOLAR GATE

      SCHOLAR GATE is a student-initiated College & Community Service Project (CSP) aimed at democratizing access to verified Indian school scholarships.

      Primary Focus: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana & All India Central Government Schemes
